AgriPath Privacy Policy
Updated November 4, 2026

Contact: hello@agripath.app

AgriPath records precise GPS locations, timestamps, GPS accuracy, spray coverage, field boundaries, equipment settings and the spray notes you enter. Active jobs and completed records are stored in SQLite on your device; preferences and recent weather are stored locally. AgriPath has no account system, advertising or analytics SDK. We do not upload your spray history or field library. The network requests AgriPath makes are described below: optional weather, maps, app updates, and problem reports you choose to send.

Background location is used only for recording you start and optional wind alerts you enable. Pausing recording stops adding points. The operating system can stop background work; after an interruption, recorded points remain available, but missing locations cannot be recovered. Wind jobs may read a recent location while alerts are enabled. Disable wind alerts to stop those checks.

When you enable weather, AgriPath sends coordinates rounded to two decimal places to the AgriPath weather gateway hosted on Cloudflare. The gateway requests forecasts for those rounded coordinates from the US National Weather Service for US locations, and from WeatherAPI.com for locations in other countries or if the National Weather Service is unavailable. Weather outside the US has not been field tested and may be unavailable in some regions. The gateway does not store or log your coordinates or spray records: it keeps no request logs, uses a hash of your IP address (or its network prefix) only for temporary rate limiting, and briefly caches forecasts by rounded coordinates. Cloudflare receives your IP address and network metadata, and the weather providers receive the gateway's request, under their own policies. Weather caching on your device is replaced by later forecasts. Weather is optional and needs internet access.

Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iOS supply the map and satellite imagery. These map services may process device, network, diagnostic and location-related information under their providers' privacy policies. Tracking-only map mode hides satellite imagery; on iOS, a standard Apple Maps base map is used and cached map details may remain visible. This mode does not remove the platform's map service from the app.

App updates: each time AgriPath starts with internet access, it asks Expo (EAS Update, Expo's update service for apps built with Expo) whether a newer version of the app's code is available, and downloads it if so; a downloaded update takes effect the next time the app starts. The request includes a random installation ID that Expo's update software creates on first launch and keeps on the device (it is not your name, an account or a phone hardware identifier, and it is replaced if you clear app storage or reinstall), the platform (Android or iOS), the app's runtime version and release channel, the IDs of the app code currently installed and built in, and the IDs of any updates that failed to start. If the update software itself failed on an earlier start, an excerpt (up to 1,024 characters) of the update system's error log is included. Expo receives your IP address and network metadata with the request. The request does not include your location, spray records, field boundaries, equipment or notes. Checking never delays the app or recording; offline, AgriPath runs the code it already has.

Exports include coverage, date, acreage, boom width, field and spray notes. Only export to destinations you trust. The receiving service controls its copy. Temporary export files are stored in the device cache.

Report a problem: AgriPath keeps a short log of recent errors on your device, the newest 50 entries, each with a time, the part of the app involved and a short technical message from which locations, links, file paths and quoted text are removed. Field names, products, rates and job notes are never logged. The log is never sent automatically. If you tap Report a problem in Settings, AgriPath opens your own email app (or, if none opens, your phone's share sheet) with a message to hello@agripath.app containing that log plus the app version and build, the update ID, your phone's make, model and Android or iOS version, your unit and weather settings, and whether a job is active. You see the whole message and can edit or delete any of it before you choose to send it, through your own email provider. We use reports only to diagnose and fix problems.

You control retention: delete completed sessions and fields in the app, or discard the active job. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local data, including preferences, the error log and export cache. Android cloud backup is disabled. Exported copies held elsewhere are not deleted by these actions.

We do not sell personal information. Service providers process requests to provide maps, optional weather and app updates. Contact hello@agripath.app for privacy questions or requests. We cannot recover records that exist only on your device.

This app is intended for adult agricultural operators. Phone GPS and forecast data are estimates, not precision guidance or an on-site wind measurement. Check GPS quality, local conditions and product instructions before spraying.

Provider policies: Google, Apple, Cloudflare, Expo, National Weather Service, WeatherAPI.com.
